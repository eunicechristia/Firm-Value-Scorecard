How to read this scorecard

  • The benchmarks come from 12 PSE-listed tourism-related firms from 2018 to 2024 (84 firm-years). They describe the sector in that period and are not targets or predictions.
  • Each check follows a significant result of the study: asset efficiency (ROA), firm size, and debt relative to equity. The study found relationships, not proof of cause and effect, and the debt result is the least stable of the three.
  • The dots show how strong the evidence is for each check: three dots for the clearest result, one for the weakest.
  • Net profit margin is not included because it had no significant relationship with price-to-book value.
  • The scorecard does not predict price-to-book value. Use it to compare a firm with the sector and to identify areas for management to review.
  • Subsector averages of return on assets are close to zero, so “All firms” or Casino and Gaming gives a more demanding comparison.
